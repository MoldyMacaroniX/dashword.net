Top player and streamer Doggie has verified the new hardest Geometry Dash level, Grief, after over 436,000 attempts and 600 hours of playtime. It also came after [two 97% fails](https://www.dashword.net/posts/doggie-dies-at-97-on-grief-top-1-hardest-demon-in-geometry-dash/).

Immediately afterwards it was [rated by RobTop](https://www.dashword.net/posts/rate-standards/) and placed at the #1 spot on the [Geometry Dash Demon List](https://www.dashword.net/posts/geometry-dash-demon-list-where-to-find-the-hardest-demons/).

# About Grief

Made by Icedcave and more, Doggie describes Grief as Icedcave's "final attempt at giving life to the level that launched his name into the community back in 2015 - Old Slaughterhouse."

Icedcave is also well known for creating [Slaughterhouse](https://www.dashword.net/posts/geometry-dash-slaughterhouse-top-1/), which itself is a former top 1 hardest level in Geometry Dash.

[Grief is a modern remake of Old Slaughterhouse](https://www.dashword.net/posts/geometry-dash-slaughterhouse-sequel-grief-verified-and-set-to-be-verified-by-doggie/), the precursor to the now famous former top 1. And now, after two years of verifying, Grief is released for everyone to try.

# The Next Top 1

In addition to Grief, [Heliopolis](https://www.dashword.net/posts/mindcap-previews-geometry-dashs-hardest-timing-demon-heliopolis/) is another upcoming hardest Geometry Dash demon. While an open verification, Zoink is currently in the lead to beat it.

Zoink also expressed on Twitter in a now deleted Tweet that Heliopolis will likely receive buffs as a result of Grief getting verified in order to claim the top 1 spot. This means Heliopolis might be the level to dethrone Grief once it is verified.

[Zoink currently has 85%](https://x.com/gdzoink/status/2105788550524400107) on the current version of Heliopolis.
